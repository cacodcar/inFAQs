What changes at a boundary?

The binding constraints can change, and so does the decision rule. In this example the optimal decision stays continuous, even as its slope changes.

Two targets, one shared limit

Choose two nonnegative allocations, x₁ and x₂, as close as possible to the requested targets θ₁ and θ₂. Each allocation has its own capacity, and their sum cannot exceed 2.

minimize ½ (x₁ − θ₁)² + ½ (x₂ − θ₂)²

subject to 0 ≤ x₁ ≤ 1.4

0 ≤ x₂ ≤ 1.2

x₁ + x₂ ≤ 2

parameters −0.6 ≤ θ₁, θ₂ ≤ 2

The negative targets make the lower-bound regions visible. Every displayed parameter pair has a feasible solution.

Each colored region has one affine rule for the optimal allocation. The solution is evaluated directly from that rule as you move the point.
